Senior Welfare

Centenarians Act — Seniors aged 80+ eligible for ₱10,000 cash incentive

Under the expanded Centenarians Act of 2016, Filipino seniors receive a ₱10,000 cash gift upon reaching age 80, with additional grants at ages 85, 90, and 95.
